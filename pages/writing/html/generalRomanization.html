1. <H> instead of <ħ> (this applies to all “pyric” consonants, e.g. <qH> instead of <qħ>)
  2. <X> instead of <χ> (this applies to all uvular consonants, e.g. <qX> instead of <qχ>)
  3. <Q> instead of <q̇> (also, due to change #1, implies <QH> instead of <q̇H> or <q̇ħ>)
  4. <ng> instead of <ŋ>
  5. <ae> instead of <æ>
  6. Vowel doubling instead of macron for long vowels (e.g. <uu> instead of <ū>, etc.)
  7. “Pyric” vowels are capitalized instead of using acute accents (e.g. <A> instead of <á>)
  8. Long “pyric” vowels are doubled and capitalized instead of using circumflexes (e.g. <OO> instead of <ô>)
